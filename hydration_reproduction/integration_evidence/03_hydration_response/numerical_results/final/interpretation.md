# Single-water response using recovered dry references

The six fixed-boundary GFN2-xTB hydrated endpoints were compared against the lowest accepted dry geometry sampled for each framework. Removing water from each relaxed hydrated geometry and reoptimizing the framework yielded three additional dry endpoints per framework. All recovered structures converged, retained the four mapped Zn donors, and preserved the nearest heavy-atom assignment of every framework H. Before reoptimization, the frozen Cartesian components were reset to the original input boundary. The largest reset was 8.8 × 10⁻⁵ Å. The lowest sampled dry endpoint for each framework originated from the free-carboxylate-water structure. Its energy was 5.83 kJ/mol below the original dry endpoint for ASR-84-0084 (A) and 1.20 kJ/mol below it for ASR-152-0152 (B). In A, an auxiliary carboxylate O2 contact shortened from 2.54 to 2.41 Å on dry recovery and its Wiberg index rose from 0.167 to 0.209. The four mapped contacts remain present. The response comparison below refers specifically to those four contacts.

Electronic association energies are in kJ/mol. The energy ordering remains placement-dependent. A is favored by 7.98 kJ/mol at the lowest sampled hydrated endpoints, down from 12.61 kJ/mol with the original dry references. B is favored for the other two matched starts. Because each dry-reference update shifts all three association energies for its framework by a constant, the within-framework spans are unchanged at 21.49 kJ/mol for A and 0.70 kJ/mol for B.

For the four mapped contacts, the largest absolute Zn–donor distance change remains larger in A (0.081 versus 0.044 Å, formerly 0.101 versus 0.046 Å). The corresponding Wiberg maximum no longer favors A (0.041 versus 0.046, formerly 0.069 versus 0.045). The difference between the updated maxima is small. Distance and Wiberg responses therefore support different comparisons, and the latter is sensitive to the dry reference. The hydrated endpoints, their water-site assignments and their equivalence screen are unchanged.

The framework deformation terms increased from −4.45 to +1.39 kJ/mol for A and from +2.12 to +3.32 kJ/mol for B. The interaction and water deformation terms are unchanged. The three terms reproduce the association energy within numerical precision. The recovered dry reference resolves the negative A deformation term within the sampled structures. The calculation probes local association in two capped cluster models and provides no framework hydrolytic stability ranking.
