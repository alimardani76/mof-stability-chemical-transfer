# Single-water response across three placements

Fixed-boundary GFN2-xTB optimizations of two Zn clusters with the same operational O2N2 coordination counts converged from three water placements each and retained all four mapped donors. The carboxylate-water start gave the lowest sampled endpoint in both ASR-84-0084 (A) and ASR-152-0152 (B). Its association energies were -58.27 and -45.66 kJ/mol, respectively. The A-minus-B contrast changes sign for the other two matched starts. A spans -58.27 to -36.78 kJ/mol, while B spans -45.66 to -44.96 kJ/mol. The lowest sampled A endpoint is therefore more favorable, but the energy ordering depends on placement. In both clusters the donor response varies by placement. The largest absolute distance and Wiberg-index changes across the three endpoints are 0.101 Å and 0.069 in A and 0.046 Å and 0.045 in B. The sampled response amplitude is larger for A, while the sign and identity of the responsive contacts vary.

A retains a water-to-oxygen hydrogen bond in all three endpoints. Its Zn-approach and bound-O starts both give O1 as the primary geometric acceptor, but their water oxygens are 1.26 Å apart and the energies differ by 4.18 kJ/mol. B has one carboxylate hydrogen-bonded endpoint and two non-hydrogen-bonded Zn-proximal endpoints with water O–Zn distances of 2.71 and 2.67 Å and Wiberg indices of 0.112 and 0.124. Those two endpoints are 0.23 kJ/mol apart and lie on approximately donor-swapped sides of the Zn site. Even after applying the donor swap that preserves the fixed/mobile atom sets, their water oxygens remain 0.38 Å apart and their water H RMSD is 0.42 Å. None of the sampled pairs meets the anchored equivalence criterion, including B after the donor swap. This is a comparison of optimized endpoints. It does not establish stationary-point equivalence by a Hessian or exhaust the accessible water configurations.

At the lowest sampled hydrated geometries, the frozen-fragment interaction energies are -55.01 (A) and -48.70 kJ/mol (B). Framework deformation terms relative to the saved dry endpoints are -4.45 and +2.12 kJ/mol. Water deformation terms are +1.19 and +0.92 kJ/mol. The three terms sum to the corresponding association energy within numerical precision. The negative framework term for A means that the extracted framework at its hydrated geometry is lower in energy than the saved dry endpoint. Thus the dry reference contributes to the 12.61 kJ/mol lowest-endpoint difference, and the saved dry endpoint is not the lowest known dry-framework geometry under this finite model. The frozen interaction contrast alone favors A by 6.31 kJ/mol. These results describe local association in two result-selected capped clusters. They do not establish a hydration free energy or a hydrolytic stability ranking.

## Calculation and figure details

All four added optimizations used the accepted GFN2-xTB 6.7.1pre, tight L-ANCopt configuration, neutral singlet, and original fixed/mobile masks. The dry clusters and isolated-water reference were reused. Frozen fragment values use the lowest accepted hydrated coordinates, with the other fragment removed and no relaxation. Hydrogen bonds are listed when H···acceptor is at most 2.50 Å and the donor–H···acceptor angle is at least 130°. Equivalence screening requires an energy difference of 0.30 kJ/mol or less, mobile framework RMSD of 0.10 Å or less, water O displacement of 0.20 Å or less, and water H RMSD of 0.30 Å or less after allowing H-label exchange. The diagram shows the actual projected local coordinates of the two lowest sampled hydrated endpoints. Distance and Wiberg panels retain all six endpoints in fixed mapped donor order. Colors denote frameworks and marker shapes denote starts.
