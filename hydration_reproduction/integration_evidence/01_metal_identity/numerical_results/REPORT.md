# Metal identity in MOF-74

The dry models were built directly from local **ASR-502-0502** (Mg) and **ASR-501-0501** (Zn). Both are three-metal rod segments with six matched ligand residues and five original O donors at the central metal. Each segment contains five carboxylates and four retained phenolates. Distal unbound phenolates were protonated and distal unbound carboxylates were replaced by aryl H. The resulting clusters are closed-shell trianions with the same capping and fixed-boundary rules. The cluster charge and finite electrostatic environment limit quantitative interpretation.

| Model | Open-metal start | Linker-O start | Lowest-energy endpoint M–O(water) (Å) | Largest sampled original central M–O change (Å) |

| Mg | −82.98 | −82.53 | 2.192 | 0.127 |

Energies are electronic association energies in kJ/mol relative to a separately relaxed dry cluster and isolated water. All four endpoints have water in the central first shell. The Mg–water distances are 2.192–2.250 Å and the Zn–water distances are 2.338–2.355 Å. The lowest sampled Mg endpoint associates 23.02 kJ/mol more strongly than its Zn counterpart. Each dry recovery ended higher than the original dry model (+8.98 for Mg and +4.14 kJ/mol for Zn), so the original references were retained. The two starts sample similar binding motifs with small differences in water orientation and donor response. The donor-resolved table matches the original O sites by coordinate alignment and ligand identity.

For Mg, the periodic PBE+D3 model reports a 2.18 Å Mg–water contact and −79.8 kJ/mol after the high-level energy correction at one water per Mg. The local FSR-443-0443 crystal has a 2.077 Å Mg–water contact. The xTB contact and literature energies have the same local binding motif and similar magnitude under different loadings and boundary treatments. Zn MOF-74 evidence for water dissociation concerns a distinct observable. P1 can use the controlled Mg/Zn contrast to put a physical interpretation on metal identity and continuous elemental features.
